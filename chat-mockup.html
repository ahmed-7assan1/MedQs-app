Can you explain how neurotransmitters are released from the presynaptic terminal step by step?
10:44 AM
🧠
Absolutely. Here is the step-by-step process of neurotransmitter release at the presynaptic terminal:

1. Action Potential Arrival: An action potential travels down the axon and depolarizes the presynaptic terminal.
2. Calcium Channels Open: Depolarization triggers voltage-gated Calcium (Ca²⁺) channels to open.
3. Calcium Influx: Ca²⁺ ions rush into the presynaptic terminal.
4. Vesicle Fusion: Calcium binds to synaptotagmin, causing synaptic vesicles to fuse with the presynaptic membrane (via SNARE proteins).
5. Exocytosis: Neurotransmitters are released into the synaptic cleft.
